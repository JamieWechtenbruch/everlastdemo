## Voice Agent: "Anna"

Anna is an inbound B2B sales agent for Kreativstrom, a KI-Projektmanagement-Plattform for agencies and marketing teams. She presents herself as a Kreativstrom employee (Vertriebsmitarbeiterin). When prospects call:

1. **Greets** warmly as a Kreativstrom employee and asks what they're interested in
2. **Qualifies** using BANT methodology (Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline) with 4+ criteria
3. **Positions** relevant Kreativstrom products based on their pain points
4. **Books** a demo appointment via Google Calendar integration

### Products
- **KI-Briefing-Automatisierung**: Intelligente Briefing-Erstellung aus Kundengesprächen
- **Smarte Timelines**: Automatische Projektplanung mit KI-Meilensteinerkennung
- **Echtzeit-Reporting**: Live-Dashboards mit automatisierten Statusberichten

### Pricing
- **Starter**: €39/Monat (bis 5 Projekte)
- **Business**: €119/Monat (bis 25 Projekte, Priority Support)
- **Enterprise**: auf Anfrage (unbegrenzt, dedizierter Account Manager)

### Qualification Criteria
- Industry & company size
- Current project management solution
- Key pain points and challenges
- Budget availability & decision timeline
- Decision-maker identification

### Lead Scoring
- **A-Lead** (7+ points): High intent, clear budget, decision-maker
- **B-Lead** (4-6 points): Moderate interest, exploring options
- **C-Lead** (0-3 points): Early stage, information gathering

### Sales Training
The agent includes professional SDR training:
- **Tonality**: Conviction-based selling, competent and human tone
- **Objection Handling**: Isolation & reframe technique for common objections (price, timing, competition)
- **Scarcity & Value**: Reference to limited onboarding slots and case study ROI
- **Closing**: Closed confirmation questions, alternative close technique

### Call Summaries
Post-call summaries are generated by Gemini AI, providing a 2-3 sentence analysis of why the call succeeded or failed, with improvement suggestions for the sales team.

## Design Decisions

1. **Gemini 2.5 Flash for LLM**: Sub-second latency with strong German language support, cost-effective for voice interactions
2. **BANT Qualification**: Industry-standard B2B qualification framework, naturally fits conversational flow
3. **Data Channel for Email**: LiveKit data channel enables browser-agent coordination without additional WebSocket infrastructure
4. **Deterministic Lead Scoring**: Rule-based scoring ensures consistent, explainable lead grades
5. **Service Account for Calendar**: No OAuth flow needed — agent books directly, simplifying the demo experience
6. **Redis-Backed Settings**: Agent reads name, company context, and prompts from Redis at session start — fully configurable from dashboard without redeployment
7. **Gemini Call Summaries**: AI-powered post-call analysis provides actionable insights instead of raw transcript snippets
8. **Employee Persona**: Agent presents as a human employee for natural conversation, only discloses AI nature when directly asked
